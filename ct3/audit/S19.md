## Deviation from the brief: the corpus is not timestamp-ordered

The brief asked for "timestamps ordered and inside the freshness window".
**Sortedness is false as stated.** In `SEED_RECORDS` order the timestamps run
2026-10-20 06:05 → 2026-10-24 15:00 except two records:

- `OPS-109` — `2025-01-03 09:00`, the deliberately stale winter record;
- `OPS-112` — `2026-10-22 06:04`, the recap of `OPS-105`, placed last among the
  `OPS` records and dated 4 minutes after the record it recaps.

What actually holds, and what the test pins: the ten-record body
(`OPS-101..108`, `OPS-110`, `OPS-111`) is strictly chronological in corpus order,
and scanning the corpus against a running maximum flags exactly
`["OPS-109", "OPS-112"]`. I pinned the narrower true claim instead of writing a
sortedness assertion that fails.

Freshness likewise needed a scoped statement. The fresh body spans 4 days and 9
hours, well under `STALE_DAYS = 90`, so a single clock makes the whole body fresh.
At `2026-10-25T00:00Z` exactly `OPS-109` is stale and no record is future-dated;
the same holds at `+7 days`, so the choice of clock is not load-bearing.

## Scope of "cites only what it was given", pinned rather than assumed

`test_a_configured_override_is_echoed_verbatim_including_an_ungiven_record` shows
that a configured `assessment_overrides` entry is returned unchanged, including a
proposal whose `source_record_id` the caller never passed. The provider does not
*invent* citations, but it is not the enforcement point for them either: rejection
belongs to `engine._validate_provider_proposals` / `MandatoryProviderBoundary`,
which treats provider output as untrusted. The guarantee in this slice is
therefore asserted on the default (fixture-free) path only, and the test that
scopes it is part of the file.

Related and **reported, not acted on** (outside this slice):
`AssessmentContextRecord.restricted` is always `False` by construction, so the
`if record.restricted: continue` filter in `assess` is only meaningful for
`Record` inputs. Restriction for the projection path is the boundary's, not this
provider's.
